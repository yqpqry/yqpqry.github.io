I am a first-year Ph.D. student at the Center on Frontiers of Computing Studies (CFCS), School of Computer Science, Peking University, advised by Prof. Xiaotie Deng.

I received my bachelor's degree from the School of Mathematical Sciences at Beihang University, where I was advised by Prof. Xiao Zhang.

My research focuses on advancing decision intelligence in complex, data-driven sequential decision-making environments. I am especially interested in empowering agents with out-of-distribution generalization and robust exploration capabilities that go beyond the limitations of offline datasets.

Methodologically, I work with large language models, offline reinforcement learning, and generative models to address challenging real-world problems, with a particular focus on auto-bidding in non-stationary auctions.

Research Interests
======

* Decision intelligence
* Offline reinforcement learning
* Out-of-distribution generalization
* Robust exploration
* Large language models and generative models
* Auto-bidding in non-stationary auctions
