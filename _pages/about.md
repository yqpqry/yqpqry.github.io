I am a first-year Ph.D. student at the Center on Frontiers of Computing Studies (CFCS), School of Computer Science, Peking University, advised by Prof. Xiaotie Deng.
I received my bachelor's degree from the School of Mathematical Sciences at Beihang University, where I was advised by Prof. Xiao Zhang.

Research Interests
======

My research focuses on advancing Decision Intelligence within complex, data-driven sequential decision-making environments. 
Specifically, I am interested in empowering agents with out-of-distribution generalization and robust exploration capabilities that surpass the limitations of offline datasets. 
Methodologically, I leverage Large Language Models (LLMs), Offline Reinforcement Learning, and Generative Models to solve challenging, real-world problems, with a particular focus on auto-bidding in non-stationary auctions.
